We Are Building

For Architects & Partners

We are seeking visionary architects, civil engineers, and investment partners who share our Pan-African ambition. Here are the technical specifications to guide your design.

Total Site Area
7 acres · 28,328 m² · approx 170m × 167m
Soil Type
Red laterite over loamy sand (Ogun/Oyo state)
Annual Rainfall
1,400–1,800mm/yr · two rainy seasons
Temperature
22°C – 33°C · Humidity 65–85%
Prevailing Wind
SW monsoon · NE harmattan (Nov–Feb)
Power System
40kW solar + 200kWh battery + PHCN backup
Water Source
80m borehole · 50,000L elevated tower · gravity-fed
Access Road
6m tarred main entry · 4m internal laterite loop

We welcome inquiries from architects, structural engineers, landscape designers, agro-investors, and development finance institutions. This project is real, the land is secured, and construction begins in 2026.
